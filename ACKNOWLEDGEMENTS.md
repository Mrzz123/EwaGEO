# Acknowledgements

EwaGEO is based on [Live-Earth-Wallpapers](https://github.com/lennart-rth/Live-Earth-Wallpapers) by lennart-rth and its contributors. Their work made this derivative possible.

Satellite imagery and related services used by the application include:

- [NOAA](https://www.noaa.gov/) and [CIRA SLIDER](https://slider.cira.colostate.edu/) for GOES and other geostationary imagery;
- [China Meteorological Administration / National Satellite Meteorological Center](https://www.nsmc.org.cn/) for FY-4B imagery;
- [Korea Meteorological Administration / National Meteorological Satellite Center](https://nmsc.kma.go.kr/) for GK-2A imagery;
- [Japan Meteorological Agency](https://www.jma.go.jp/) for Himawari imagery;
- [NASA Solar Dynamics Observatory](https://sdo.gsfc.nasa.gov/);
- [NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/astropix.html);
- [EUMETSAT](https://view.eumetsat.int/productviewer?v=default), where applicable, under the provider's stated terms.

The default space background is a cropped widescreen adaptation of [The Hockey Stick Galaxy](https://esahubble.org/images/potw1731a/) (`GSFC_20171208_Archive_e000012`). Credit: **ESA/Hubble & NASA**. ESA/Hubble makes the source available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence.

Location names and representative coordinates are derived from the [Countries States Cities Database](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database), licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0. The bundled detailed notice is at `liewa/liewa_cli/recources/location-data-NOTICE.txt`.
